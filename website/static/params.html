Why these are different factories

Every circuit listed here consumes the same n, protects the same k outputs and reaches the same distance d. They differ in what they deposit: no two gates on this page are related by a change of output basis and Clifford corrections, so each row prepares a genuinely different magic state at the same price.

Every number is re-derived from the factory’s own circuit.
