How to read an entry

A factory is a circuit of n π/4 parity rotations on N = k + r wires. Each rotation consumes one noisy T state. The first k wires are outputs; the other r are checks, measured and postselected on. Stack the rotations into a binary matrix — one row per wire, one column per rotation — and that matrix is the whole circuit. The gate it deposits on the outputs is read off the row overlaps, and the circuit is a valid factory exactly when no phase lands on a check wire.

n
rotations, i.e. noisy T states consumed per run
k
output wires, indexed 0…k−1
d
circuit distance: the least number of faulty rotations that is both undetected and damaging. Exact means proved and witnessed; otherwise it is a proved lower bound
N, r
total wires, and the r = N − k postselected checks
T-count
exact minimal T-count of the deposited gate, where computable
γ
log(n/k)/log d — the yield exponent counted in output wires
γT
log(n/T-count)/log d — the same, counted in T-cost
γρ
log(n/Vex)/log d — counted in extractable T states; the fair comparison across gates. Lower is better for all three

One row per magic state. Two circuits with the same n, k and d are one row when their output gates differ only by an invertible change of output basis and diagonal Clifford corrections (the GL(k,2), or CNOT+S, equivalence): they prepare the same magic state, so T0·T1 and T0·CS01 are one class. The stored circuit is one representative. Circuits at different distances are always different rows.

About, citing and contributing

Where the factories come from

  • Exhaustive classification of every generalised triorthogonal protocol with up to 54 inputs (Wills, Jain and Singh).
  • AI-assisted and symmetry-constrained SAT searches for larger and better protocols (Jain, Wills and Singh).
  • The published literature, credited to its papers.
  • Community contributions, verified to the same bar.

How to cite

If you use a factory, cite the papers listed on its page. For the catalogue itself:

    Contribute a factory

    Protocols can be submitted in any format — a paper, matrices, code or a notebook. They are verified to the same bar as every row here and credited to their authors.
