What the catalogue offers

Search
Find factories by parameters, gate, distance or source.
A page per factory
Its circuit as a matrix, its figures of merit, its distance, and its original source.
CNOT and S conversion
Test whether another output gate is reachable with CNOT and S gates, and get the circuit that does it. Or list every gate it reaches.
Downloads
Each factory as JSON, CSV or a numpy matrix. The whole catalogue as CSV and JSON.
Credit
Each factory names the paper that found it or the person who contributed it. All of them are on the references page.
