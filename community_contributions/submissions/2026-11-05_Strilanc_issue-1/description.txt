AFAIK it doesn't correspond to a code, because the operators being phased don't all commute with each other. It starts by preparing a fault tolerant CCZ state using Jones' 8T -> CCZ construction, and then applies Cliffords that turn the CCZ state into a state that differs from a stabilizer state by 4 T gates and undoes two of them in a way that leaves a symptom on one qubit with the other two qubits becoming the T outputs.

This is also the basis for the 8T -> 2T factory with quadratic suppression catalyzed by a T state, by using the catalyst to reliably undo one of the 4 T gates after the Clifford transform which then gives an extra T output that becomes the new catalyst.
